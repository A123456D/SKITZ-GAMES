**RITE** is a ranked honesty fitness app.

- Log food and training against your daily targets
- Heat moves with the day; rank is the climb
- Ash is the coach — no pep-talk mascot
- Everything stays in your browser. Download a backup if you switch phones.

**Free** in the browser. On Android Chrome or desktop, tap **Install app**. On iPhone, use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
