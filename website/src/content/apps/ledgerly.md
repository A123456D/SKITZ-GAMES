**Ledgerly** is a local-first invoice app for freelancers.

- Create branded invoices with live A4 preview
- South African defaults (ZAR + 15% VAT) — override anytime
- Upload Canva letterheads or pick a built-in template
- Download PDF, send from the app, install as a home-screen app
- Clients, catalog, and drafts stay in your browser (auto-backups on this device)

**Free to use** in the browser. On Android/Chrome or desktop, tap **Install app**. On iPhone, use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
