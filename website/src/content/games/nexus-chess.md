**Nexus Chess** is SKITZ zone-control chess: win by holding the center Nexus with your King for a full round, or by capturing theirs while it stands inside. Earn mana to cast Aegis, Overdrive, or Swap before your move. Play the computer at any rating, flip boards (Nexus / Forge / Classic), and learn the loop in a short first-run tutorial.

**Free to play** online. **Free to install** on Android via Chrome (Install app).
