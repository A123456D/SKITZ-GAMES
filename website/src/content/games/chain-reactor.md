**Chain Reactor** is a SKITZ cyberpunk card battler: drag nodes onto a shared 3×4 grid, trigger orthogonal energy beams, capture enemy tiles, and win by the highest controlled power after six rounds.

**Free to play** online. Choose Volt (Flood), Prismatic (Redirect), or Void (Invert) and duel a tactical AI — or clear the Campaign districts and today’s Daily Chain challenge.

Tap **Play** to jump in. After your first win, try **Daily** for a shareable score.
