**Gravity Drift** is a SKITZ spin on Tetris in a circular well. Claim a username, aim around the rim, release, and pack tetrominoes toward the glowing core. Full rings explode and collapse inward. The stack reaching the rim is a core breach.

**Free to play** online. Scores rank on the world board under your username. Install as an app after one visit for offline play.
