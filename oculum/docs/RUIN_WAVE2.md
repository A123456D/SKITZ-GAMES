## Vespera — Low Tempt / Low Devour

- **Veiled:** When you Tempt an enemy Figure on **Low**, gain **1 Sight**.
- **Revelation:** Tempt the enemy Figure here if able (does not spend once/window).
- **While Witnessed on Low:** Your Devour against a Branded enemy on Low deals **+1 Will** (2 Will total if Witnessed).
- **Tempt / Brand / Devour:** shared.

*Blackout:* Low Sight-on-Tempt + Low Devour spike — not Liaison’s Witness tax, not Spire Hunger’s High free-Witness / High Devour, not Vow’s global Brand Sight.

## Thorn Font — Brand / Tempt bank

- **Site.** When an enemy Figure **here** becomes **Branded**, gain **1 Sight**.
- When you **Tempt** an enemy Figure **here**, gain **1 Sight**.

*Blackout:* Lane Sight bank on Brand and Tempt — not Desire Altar’s free Tempt / action-preserve, not Vow’s global Brand Sight.

## Want Urn — Continuity Tempt

- **Vessel.** On play, tuck a Figure from hand as Inhabitant if able.
- **Revelation:** Tempt the enemy Figure here if able; otherwise Brand a Witnessed enemy Figure here if able.
- **Fall:** Tempt an enemy Veiled Figure if able; otherwise gain **1 Sight**.

*Blackout:* Tempt continuity on Rev/Fall — not Unwrite’s cash/Blind fork.

## Horn Charm — Brand / Devour graft

- **Graft:** +1 power while host Witnessed.
- When an enemy Figure on the host’s altitude becomes **Branded**, gain **1 Sight**.
- When you **Devour** a Branded enemy on the host’s altitude, gain **1 Sight**.

*Blackout:* Lane Brand + Devour Sight graft — not Thorn Font’s Site bank, not Vow’s global Brand Sight.

## Invite the Look — Tempt / Brand / cash

- Choose an altitude with an enemy Figure:
  - If **Veiled** and not Tempted: **Tempt** it (does not spend once/window).
  - If **Veiled** and Tempted: **Brand** it (skips Witness).
  - If **Witnessed** and Branded: gain **1 Sight**.
  - If **Witnessed** and unbranded: **Brand** it.

*Blackout:* Cheap kit fork — Tempt, force Brand on bait, cash Brand, or Brand face-up — not Unwrite’s Blind fork.
