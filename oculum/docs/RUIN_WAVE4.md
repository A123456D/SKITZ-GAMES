## Veloth — Sovereign

- **SOVEREIGN** (≤1 in Constructed).
- **Veiled:** Whenever you **Tempt** an enemy Figure, gain **1 Sight**.
- **Revelation:** Tempt an enemy Veiled Figure if able. Then Brand a Tempted enemy Figure if able. If you control **2 or more** Branded enemy Figures after this, deal **1 Will** and gain **1 Sight**.
- **While Witnessed:** Your Devours against **Witnessed** Branded enemies deal **+1 Will**.
- **Fall:** Gain **1 Sight**.

*Blackout vs Vow / Spire / Full Devour:* Vow = Brand → Sight. Spire = High free-Witness bait + High Devour. Full Devour = temporary global spike. Veloth = Tempt → Sight engine + Rev Tempt/Brand + 2-Brand payoff + permanent Witnessed Devour aura.

## Lace Charm — Tempt / Devour Blind graft

- **Graft:** +1 power while host Witnessed.
- When an enemy Figure on the host’s altitude becomes **Tempted**, gain **1 Sight**.
- When you **Devour** a Witnessed Branded enemy on the host’s altitude (dealing Will), **Blind** that altitude this turn if an enemy is there.

*Blackout vs Horn Charm:* Horn = Brand Sight + Devour Sight. Lace = Tempt Sight + Devour **Blind** (deny, not bank).

## Hunger Urn — Continuity Tempt

- **Vessel.** On play, tuck a Figure from hand as Inhabitant if able.
- **Revelation:** Tempt an enemy Veiled Figure if able; otherwise gain **2 Sight**.
- **Fall:** If you control a Branded enemy Figure, gain **1 Sight**; otherwise Tempt an enemy Veiled Figure if able.

*Blackout vs Want Urn:* Want = Rev Tempt-here / Brand-here; Fall Tempt / Sight. Hunger = Rev Tempt-anywhere / Sight dump; Fall Brand-bank Sight / Tempt.

## Wantwell — Brand power + Sight deny

- **Site.** While a **Branded** enemy Figure is here, your Figure here has **+1 power**.
- When an enemy Figure here becomes **Branded**, the opponent loses **1 Sight** if able.

*Blackout vs Thorn Font / Lace Gallery:* Font = your Sight on Tempt/Brand. Gallery = anthem elsewhere + chain Tempt. Wantwell = **local power** + **taxes rival Sight** on Brand here.

## Last Devour — Closer

- Choose an altitude with an enemy Figure:
  - If **Veiled** and not Tempted: **Tempt** it (does not spend once/window).
  - If **Veiled** and Tempted: **Brand** it.
  - If **Witnessed** and Branded: deal **2 Will**.
  - If **Witnessed** and unbranded: **Brand** it.

*Blackout vs Invite / Full Devour / Unwrite:* Invite = 1E Tempt/Brand/cash Sight. Full Devour = global Pass spike. Unwrite = cash Brand Sight or Blind. Last Devour = Tempt/Brand fork or **2 Will** closer.
