## Thorncrown — High Brand densify

- **Veiled:** While on **High**, whenever an enemy Figure becomes **Branded** in another altitude, gain **1 Sight**.
- **Revelation:** If on High, Brand a Tempted enemy Figure if able; otherwise Tempt the enemy Figure here if able.
- **While Witnessed on High:** Enemy Witness and Gaze on High cost **+1 Sight** unless the target is Tempted.
- **Tempt / Brand / Devour:** shared.

*Blackout vs Spire Hunger / Liaison:* Spire = free Witness elsewhere + High Devour Will + Rev Brand Tempted. Liaison = any-lane Veiled Witness tax. Thorncrown = High Brand Sight (elsewhere) + Rev Brand-or-Tempt + **Witnessed** High tax unless Tempted.

## Siltthorn — Low Blind / tax

- **Veiled:** Hold on **Low** while you control a **Branded** enemy Figure → **Blind** Low this turn.
- **Revelation:** If on Low, Tempt the enemy Figure here if able; otherwise gain **1 Sight**.
- **While Witnessed on Low:** Enemy Witness and Gaze on Low cost **+1 Sight**.
- **Tempt / Brand / Devour:** shared.

*Blackout vs Vespera:* Vespera = Tempt Sight + Low Devour spike. Siltthorn = Hold Blind off Brand board + flat Witnessed Low tax (no Tempt relax).

## Brandlace — Mid Tempt escort

- **Veiled:** The first time each window you **Tempt**, gain **1 Sight**.
- **Revelation:** Tempt the enemy Figure on Mid if able; otherwise Brand a Witnessed enemy Figure on Mid if able.
- **Tempt / Brand / Devour:** shared.

*Blackout vs Crimson Vow / Invite:* Vow = Brand anywhere → Sight. Invite = cheap Tempt/Brand fork. Brandlace = Tempt → Sight (first/window) + Mid Tempt/Brand Rev.

## Lace Gallery — Brand anthem

- **Site.** While a **Branded** enemy Figure is here, your other Figures have **+1 power**.
- When an enemy Figure here becomes **Branded**, Tempt an enemy Veiled Figure in another altitude if able (does not spend once/window).

*Blackout vs Thorn Font / Desire Altar:* Font = Sight on Tempt/Brand here. Altar = free Tempt. Gallery = **anthem** power + chain Tempt elsewhere on Brand.

## Full Devour — Devour surge

- Gain **1 Sight**.
- Until Resolve: your Devours against **Witnessed** Branded enemies deal **+1 Will**.

*Blackout vs Unwrite / Invite:* Unwrite = cash Brand or Blind. Invite = Tempt/Brand now. Full Devour = global temporary Devour Will spike.

## Pack sketch

Brandlace mints Sight off Tempts → Lace Gallery turns a Branded lane into board power + chain Tempt → Full Devour spikes Pass Will → Thorncrown banks Brand Sight from High and taxes High looks → Siltthorn Blinds/taxes Low once Brands are down.
