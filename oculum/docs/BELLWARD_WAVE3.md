# Bellward Toll — Wave 3 (Trap Tax lanes)

Three Figures + Site + Rite. Cast: three male humans — no glam stack. Placeholders until art greenlight.

| Card | Type | Cost | Role |
|------|------|------|------|
| **Highcliff Ringer** | Figure | 3E / 2W · V2 / W4 | High Toll planter |
| **Lowcloth Warden** | Figure | 2E / 1W · V2 / W3 | Low Hold Blind |
| **Rope Auditor** | Figure | 3E / 2W · V1 / W4 | Witness/Lure tax on Tolled |
| **Banner Bellwalk** | Site | 2E | Lure Sight |
| **Ring Out** | Rite | 2E | Toll or Resonance+Sight |

## Highcliff Ringer — High planter
- **Revelation:** Toll High if able; else +1 Sight.
- Kit occupancy covers Tolled-lane power.

## Lowcloth Warden — Low specialist
- **Veiled:** Hold on Low while Low Tolled → Blind Low.
- **Revelation:** Toll Low; if already Tolled, draw 1.

## Rope Auditor — tax
- **Veiled:** First enemy Witness/Lure on a Tolled altitude each turn costs them +1 Sight.
- **Revelation:** Toll Mid if able; else draw 1.

## Banner Bellwalk — Lure bank
- **Site.** Lure → +1 Sight.

## Ring Out — flex rite
- Choose an altitude: if Tolled, Resonance + Sight; else Toll + Resonance.
