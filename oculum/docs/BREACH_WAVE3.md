## Shared grammar

Witnessed Scar Breach Figures: contested Open Resolve win → **+1 Will** Breach — **up to twice per side per Resolve**. Veiled / empty / Veiled-foe wins do not.  
**Overexpose:** shared (see `docs/CRAFT_DENSITY.md`).  
Highscar (W1) already owns **High Breach spike** (+1 Will beyond shared Breach while Witnessed on High). Wave 3 High must not clone that rider.

## Anti-overlap

No Stain / Forced Exposed · no Stance / Wager · no Toll / Lure / Resonance · no Eclipse seeding · no Challenge/Duel.  
Hate soft-touch: Lowscar Blind on Hold only (≤1).

## Cliffbrand Captain — High Open captain

- **Veiled:** While on High, whenever you Witness a friendly Figure elsewhere, gain 1 Sight.
- **Revelation:** If on High, Blind High this turn if an enemy is there; otherwise draw 1.
- **While Witnessed on High:** When this wins Resolve, draw 1 (in addition to shared Breach — **not** extra Breach Will; that’s Highscar’s job).
- **Overexpose:** shared; **on High, opponent draws 1**.

*Blackout vs Highscar:* Highscar = Veiled +1 power on High + Witnessed High **Breach spike** + High Overexpose **self Will**. Captain = Sight off remote Opens + Blind/draw Rev + Witnessed High **draw** on win + High Overexpose **opponent draw**.

## Lowscar Warden — Low Open ward

- **Veiled:** Hold on Low while you control another Witnessed friendly Figure → Blind Low this turn.
- **Revelation:** If on Low, draw 1; otherwise gain 1 Sight.
- **While Witnessed on Low:** Enemy Witness and Gaze on Low cost +1 Sight.

*Blackout:* Low Hold Blind needs your board already Open (Breach fantasy). Witnessed Low taxes rival looking — not Toll sticky mark.

## Ember Herald — Breach Sight mint

- **Veiled:** The first time each Resolve you deal Breach Will, gain 1 Sight.
- **Revelation:** Witness another friendly Veiled Figure paying 1 less Sight (min 0). If you control no other Veiled Figure, gain 2 Sight instead.
- **Breach:** shared.

*Blackout vs Ember Banner:* Banner = Hold Sight + Rev count Witnessed + sightYield. Herald = Sight **from Breach deals** + Rev discounted Open / fail-into-Sight.

## Banner Drill — Open anthem bank

- **Site.** While a friendly Witnessed Figure is here, your other Witnessed Figures have +1 power.
- When a friendly Figure here becomes Witnessed, gain 1 Sight.

*Blackout vs Scarforge:* Scarforge = Sight on Open here + Sight on any Witnessed win here. Drill = **anthem** for other Opens + Sight on Open here only (no win Sight).

## Full Breach — Breach surge

- Gain 1 Sight.
- Until Resolve: your Witnessed Scar Breach Figures deal **+1 Will** Breach in addition to shared Breach on each Breach payout this Resolve (temporary Breach 2).
- Until Resolve: the first Overexpose you take deals **1 Will to you** (surge is double-edged).

*Blackout vs Breach Order:* Order = cheap Open **or** 1 Will now. Full Breach = global temporary Breach surge + Sight + Overexpose sting — no forced Witness.

## Pack sketch

Herald mints Sight off Breach hits → Full Breach doubles Breach for a Resolve → Banner Drill turns one Open lane into board-wide Witnessed power → Captain Opens High and draws on wins → Lowscar Blinds/taxes Low once your board is face-up.
