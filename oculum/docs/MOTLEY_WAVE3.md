# Motley Masquerade — Wave 3 (lanes + Favor)

Three Figures + Site + Rite. **Favor** (0–3) is Cash / Final Raise / Mirrorhall fuel — not a Wager ante.

| Card | Type | Cost | Role |
|------|------|------|------|
| **Spire Caprice** | Figure | 3E / 2W · V2 / W4 | High specialist |
| **Pit Capper** | Figure | 2E / 1W · V2 / W3 | Low specialist |
| **Favor Broker** | Figure | 3E / 2W · V1 / W4 | Favor mint |
| **Gala Mirrorhall** | Site | 2E | Favor power bank |
| **Gala Call** | Rite | 2E | Favor + Gala surge |

## Favor (shared)

- Cap **3**. Income: **Cash** (and card effects) — max **+1 Favor/turn** from Cash income. Stance B Hold no longer mints Favor.
- Spend: **Final Raise** Eclipse (1 Favor). Mirrorhall buffs while you have Favor.
- Free Wager never grants Favor. All intentional Wagers ante **Sight**.
- School Trick Eclipse requires a **paid** ante (Free Wager does not count).

## Spire Caprice — High specialist
- **Veiled:** On High + Wagered → +1 power. May Wager (Sight).
- **Revelation:** If on High, Free Wager + gain 1 Sight.
- **Cash:** Blind Mid.

## Pit Capper — Low specialist
- **Veiled:** Hold on Low while you control a Wagered Figure → Blind Low.
- **Revelation:** Free Wager; if on Low, draw 1.
- **Bust:** gain 1 Favor (income rule).

## Favor Broker — Favor mint
- **Veiled:** Wager ante **1 Sight**. Win Veiled + Stance B + Wagered → +1 Sight.
- **Revelation:** Switch Stance; if became B, gain 1 Favor.
- **Cash:** draw 1 (Sight refund).

## Gala Mirrorhall — Favor power bank
- **Site.** While you have Favor, Stance B Figures +1 power.
- When a friendly Figure here Cashes, gain 1 Sight.

## Gala Call — Gala surge
- Gain 1 Favor. Until Resolve, Stance B Figures +1 power.

## Pack sketch
Broker / Call mint Favor → Mirrorhall turns Favor into board power → Caprice all-ins High → Capper Blinds Low on Hold → Favor spent on Trick Eclipse / Final Raise.
