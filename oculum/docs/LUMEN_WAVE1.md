# Lumen Host — Wave 1

**Verb:** Radiance · **Kit:** Halo · Blaze · Fall (Sustain)

Tagline: *The Eye’s angels burn brightest when Named — and cannot stay Named for free.*

## Shared craft rules

1. **Halo** — When you Witness your own Lumen Figure (not Gaze), it becomes Halo’d (+1 power while Witnessed).
2. **Blaze** — When you Pass, each Halo’d Figure Blazes: 1 Will if the lane has an enemy (else +1 Sight). Cards may print stronger Blaze.
3. **Sustain / Fall** — After Blaze, unless you Sustained that Figure this window (1 Sight, or free once via Lumen Shrine), it Re-Veils and loses Halo.
