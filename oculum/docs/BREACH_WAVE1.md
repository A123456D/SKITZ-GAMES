# Scar Breach — Wave 1 (identity pass)

Five dual-mode Figures. **Breach** = Witnessed Resolve wins deal +1 Will after soft Resolve.  
**Open** = pay Sight to Witness so Breach turns on.  
**Overexpose** = if you lose Resolve while Witnessed after Opening this round, lose 1 Sight (shared; see `docs/CRAFT_DENSITY.md`).  
**Status:** Overexpose **in engine + CardDefs**; **Wave 1 open-wound faces** shipping (art law in `ART_STYLE.md` / `CRAFTS.md`).

Shared grammar (all Wave 1 Figures, heresy `breach`):

- While **Witnessed**, when this wins Resolve **against a Witnessed enemy occupant**: deal **+1 Will** Breach (in addition to shared soft Resolve damage) — **up to twice per side per Resolve**. Riders (Highscar / Full Breach / Skaroth aura) add at most **+1** more on each payout (max **2** Breach Will per payout).
- While **Veiled**, winning an **empty** lane, or winning into a **Veiled** foe: Resolve wins do **not** Breach.
- **Overexpose:** first loss each Resolve while Witnessed, if Opened since previous Resolve → controller loses 1 Sight.

## Anti-overlap (Wave 1)

No Stain / Forced Exposed · no Stance / Wager · no Toll / Lure / Resonance · no Eclipse seeding · no Challenge/Duel marks.

## Rivet Vanguard — Open frontline

- **Veiled:** When this wins Resolve while Veiled, gain 1 Sight.
- **Revelation:** Witness another friendly Veiled Figure paying 1 less Sight (min 0). If you did, gain 1 Sight.
- **Breach:** shared.
- **Overexpose:** shared (lose 1 Sight) — Wave 1 teacher line.

*Blackout:* Veiled win → Sight (march fuel). Revelation discounted Open ally (unique Open choir beat). Open risk taught via Overexpose.

## Ember Banner — Sight battery

- **Veiled:** When this Holds, gain 1 Sight.
- **Revelation:** Gain 1 Sight for each Witnessed friendly Figure you control (including this), max 3.
- **While Witnessed:** +1 Sight/turn (printed Sight/turn 1).
- **Breach:** shared.

*Blackout:* Hold → Sight (not win-based). Revelation scales on how Open the board already is.

## Highscar Lancer — High lance

- **Veiled:** While on High, +1 power.
- **Revelation:** If on High, draw 1; otherwise gain 1 Sight.
- **While Witnessed on High:** When this wins Resolve, deal **+1 Will** in addition to shared Breach (High Open spike = Breach 2 total rider Will on that win).
- **Breach:** shared (+ the High rider above).
- **Overexpose:** shared; **on High, also take 1 Will**.

*Blackout:* Altitude-locked power + High-only draw + High Breach spike + High Overexpose Will sting. Not a generic +1.

## Scarsteel Cleaver — Mid Open engine

- **Veiled:** Whenever another friendly Figure becomes Witnessed, gain 1 Sight.
- **Revelation:** Draw 1. Then: you may Witness another friendly Veiled Figure paying 1 less Sight (min 0).
- **Breach:** shared.

*Blackout:* Ally-Open → Sight network. Revelation Mid-tempo draw + discounted Open (not free like Vanguard).

## Slag Reaper — Strain execute

- **Veiled:** The first time each turn an enemy Figure becomes Strained, draw 1.
- **Revelation:** If any enemy Figure is Strained, deal 1 Will.
- **While Witnessed:** If a Strained enemy Figure here loses Resolve, they **Fall** (Unmake) — Strain finish, not Ink Stain.
- **Breach:** shared.

*Blackout:* Pays off shared Unmake clock; execute line is Strain/Fall, never Stain.

## Pack sketch

Ember Holds / yields Sight → Rivet marches Veiled then Opens an ally at discount → Scarsteel discounts further Opens and banks Sight off ally Witness → Highscar Opens on High for Breach spike → Slag finishes Strained blockers so Breach lanes stay clear.
