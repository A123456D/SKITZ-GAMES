## Shared grammar

Witnessed Scar Breach Figures: contested Open Resolve win → **+1 Will** Breach — **up to twice per side per Resolve**. Veiled / empty / Veiled-foe wins do not.  
**Overexpose:** shared (see `docs/CRAFT_DENSITY.md`).  
Constructed: **at most one Sovereign**.

## Anti-overlap

No Stain / Forced Exposed · no Stance / Wager · no Toll / Lure / Resonance · no Eclipse Debt · no Challenge/Duel.

## Skaroth — Sovereign

- **SOVEREIGN** (≤1 in Constructed).
- **Veiled:** Whenever a friendly Figure becomes Witnessed, gain 1 Sight. The first time each Resolve you deal Breach Will, this has +1 power until end of that Resolve.
- **Revelation:** Witness another friendly Veiled Figure paying 1 less Sight (min 0) if able. If you control 2 or more Witnessed Figures after this (including this), deal 1 Will and draw 1.
- **While Witnessed:** Your **other** Scar Breach Figures deal **+1 Will** Breach on Resolve Breach payouts (in addition to shared Breach). Skaroth’s own wins use shared Breach only (plus Highscar/Full Breach riders if applicable).
- **Overexpose:** shared; **opponent gains 1 Sight**.
- **Fall:** Draw 1.

*Blackout:* Legend wants the board Open and turns **allied** Breach lanes into double rider Will — and risks feeding Sight on Overexpose. Not Ink Forced Exposed, not Motley Cash, not Toll Resonance.

## Eyebrand Charm — Breach Sight graft

- **Graft:** +1 power while host Witnessed.
- When host wins Resolve while Witnessed, gain 1 Sight. If that altitude is High, also draw 1.

*Blackout vs Rivet Charm:* Rivet = Sight on **becoming** Witnessed + draw on Witnessed win. Eyebrand = Sight on Witnessed win + High draw — no Open-on-Witness Sight (avoids double Charm clone).

## Ash Urn — Continuity Open

- **Vessel.** On play, tuck a Figure from hand as Inhabitant if able.
- **Revelation:** Witness a friendly Veiled Figure for free if able; otherwise gain 2 Sight.
- **Fall:** Deal 1 Will if you control a Witnessed Figure; otherwise Witness a friendly Veiled Figure for free if able.

*Blackout vs Iron Urn:* Iron = Rev **+2 Sight**, Fall free-Witness / Sight. Ash = Rev **free-Witness first**, Fall **Will if Open else free-Witness**.

## Openwell — Open power + Sight deny

- **Site.** Witnessed Figures here have +1 power.
- When a friendly Figure here becomes Witnessed, the opponent loses 1 Sight if able.

*Blackout vs Scarforge / Banner Drill:* Scarforge banks your Sight; Drill anthems other lanes; Openwell is **local power** + **taxes rival Sight** on Open here.

## Last Breach — Closer

- Choose an altitude with your Figure:
  - If **Veiled:** Witness it paying 1 less Sight (min 0). If that Figure Overexposes this Resolve, draw 1.
  - If **already Witnessed:** deal 2 Will.

*Blackout vs Breach Order / Full Breach:* Order = cheap Open **or** 1 Will. Full Breach = global temporary Breach 2 + Overexpose sting. Last Breach = **discounted Open** (with Overexpose consolation draw) or **2 Will** closer — one lane, hard commit.

## Pack sketch

Eyebrand / Openwell pay Open lanes → Ash Urn keeps Open after Falls → Last Breach discounts Open or punches 2 Will → Skaroth Revelation Opens the line and Witnessed aura doubles **allied** Breach hits.
