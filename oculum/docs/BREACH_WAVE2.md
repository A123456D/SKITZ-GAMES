# Scar Breach — Wave 2 (Open / Breach support pack)

Five kit tools. Wave 1 keeps lane roles; Wave 2 **funds Open** (Sight) and **pays Breach**.  
**Status:** design lock — Wave 2 open-wound faces shipping with Wave 1 style.

| Card | Type | Cost | Role | Cast / object |
|------|------|------|------|----------------|
| **Ashcoil Blade** | Figure | 3E / 2W · V2 / W3 | Open escort | ♂ human |
| **Scarforge** | Site | 2E | Open bank | forge landmark |
| **Iron Urn** | Vessel | 3E / 2W · V1 / W3 | Continuity Open | war urn |
| **Rivet Charm** | Relic | 2E · Graft | Breach graft | iron charm |
| **Breach Order** | Rite | 1E | Open / spike | edict |

## Shared grammar

Witnessed Scar Breach Figures: contested Open Resolve win → **+1 Will** Breach after soft Resolve — **up to twice per side per Resolve**. Veiled / empty / Veiled-foe wins do not Breach.  
**Overexpose:** lose Resolve while Witnessed after Opening this round → lose 1 Sight (shared). See `docs/CRAFT_DENSITY.md`.

## Anti-overlap (Wave 2)

No Stain / Forced Exposed · no Stance / Wager · no Toll / Lure / Resonance · no Eclipse seeding · no Challenge/Duel marks.  
Hate vs one rival: ≤1 soft touch (none required here).

## Ashcoil Blade — Open escort

- **Veiled:** Whenever another friendly Figure becomes Witnessed, this has +1 power until Resolve (max +2). The first Overexpose you take each Resolve, this gains +1 power until Resolve (same max +2 pool).
- **Revelation:** Gain 1 Sight. You may Witness a friendly Veiled Figure paying 1 less Sight (min 0).
- **Breach:** shared.

*Blackout vs Wave 1:* Cleaver gains **Sight** off ally Open; Blade gains **power** off ally Open / Overexpose risk and Rev discounts Open (Cleaver also discounts — differentiate Rev): Blade Rev is Sight + discount; Cleaver Rev is **draw** + discount.

## Scarforge — Open bank

- **Site.** When a friendly Figure here becomes Witnessed, gain 1 Sight.
- When a friendly Witnessed Figure here wins Resolve (Breach or not), gain 1 Sight.

*Blackout:* Site banks Open and Resolve on this lane — not Hold Sight (Ember), not free Witness (Rivet).

## Iron Urn — Continuity Open

- **Vessel.** On play, tuck a Figure from hand as Inhabitant if able.
- **Revelation:** Witness this for free if still Veiled after enter… Vessels — check how other crafts do it. Toll Urn: Revelation Tolls. Motley Urn: Free Wager.
- **Revelation:** Gain 2 Sight.
- **Fall:** Witness a friendly Veiled Figure for free if able; otherwise gain 1 Sight.

*Blackout:* Fall Open assist (Rivet does Rev Open; Urn does Fall Open). Revelation is raw Sight dump for Open turns.

## Rivet Charm — Breach graft

- **Graft:** +1 power while host Witnessed.
- When host becomes Witnessed, gain 1 Sight.
- When host wins Resolve while Witnessed, draw 1 (once per Resolve).

*Blackout:* Pays Open (Sight on Witness) and Breach (draw on Witnessed win) — not Stance/Cash (Coinface), not Toll/Resonance (Bellcord).

## Breach Order — Open / spike

- Choose an altitude with your Figure:
  - If **Veiled:** Witness it paying 1 less Sight (min 0).
  - If **already Witnessed:** deal 1 Will instead (cast spike — not a duel mark).

*Blackout:* Rite is either discounted Open or flat Will if already Open — never Toll, never Stain, never Wager ante.

## Pack sketch

Scarforge banks Sight when you Open / win on its lane → Rivet Charm turns host Open into Sight and Breach wins into cards → Breach Order Opens cheap or spikes if already Open → Ashcoil rides ally Opens into power → Iron Urn dumps Sight on Rev and Opens on Fall so the warband stays face-up.
