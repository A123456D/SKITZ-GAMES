# Motley Masquerade — Wave 4 (closing pack)

Motley **20/20 complete**. Sovereign + Blind payoff + Vessel + Bust bank + closing rite.

| Card | Type | Cost | Role |
|------|------|------|------|
| **Lady Masque** | Figure · Sovereign | 5E / 3W · V3 / W7 | Cash lord |
| **Blindfold Charm** | Relic | 2E · Graft | Blind payoff |
| **Carnival Urn** | Vessel | 3E / 2W · V1 / W3 | Continuity / Free Wager |
| **Antewell** | Site | 2E | Bust bank + Wager power |
| **Final Raise** | Rite | 2E | Favor→Eclipse / paid ante Wager |

## Lady Masque — Sovereign
- **Veiled:** Friendly Cash → +1 Sight.
- **Revelation:** Free Wager this. If 2+ Wagered Figures, +1 Eclipse and draw 1.
- **Witnessed:** Friendly Cash → +1 Favor. Cash 2+ in one Resolve while Witnessed → +1 Eclipse.
- **Fall:** Draw 1.

## Blindfold Charm — Blind payoff
- **Graft:** +1 while Witnessed.
- Blind while host Wagered → +1 Sight; if Mid, draw 1.

## Carnival Urn — Continuity
- Tuck Inhabitant on play.
- **Revelation:** Switch Stance; Free Wager another.
- **Fall:** Free Wager another; if you have Favor, +1 Sight.

## Antewell — Bust bank
- Friendly Bust here → draw 1.
- Wagered Figures here +1 power.

## Final Raise — Closer
- If target is Wagered: spend 1 Favor → +1 Eclipse; else draw 1.
- Else ante 1 Sight to Wager it if able; otherwise draw 1.
