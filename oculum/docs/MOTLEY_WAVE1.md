## Wager (shared)

- **Wager:** Veiled Figure, once/turn — ante **1 Sight** → Wagered.
- **Free Wager:** card effect; no ante (`wagerAntePaid` false).
- **Cash:** win Resolve while Veiled + Wagered → refund ante if paid + Cash rider.
- **Bust:** lose Resolve or Forced Exposed while Wagered → no refund + Bust rider.
- Own Witness while Wagered → **Fold** (clear, no refund). Enemy Gaze on Wagered → **Bust**.

## Whitecard Mummer — Ante opener
- **Veiled:** Hold → enter Stance B. May Wager.
- **Revelation:** Enter Stance B. If Wagered, gain 1 Sight.
- **Cash:** draw 1.

## Diamond Widow — High-roller
- **Veiled:** Win while Veiled + Stance B + Wagered → draw 2. May Wager.
- **Revelation:** Switch Stance.
- **Bust:** Blind this altitude.

## Split-Hymn Cantor — Table dealer
- **Veiled:** Hold → Switch Stance on another friendly.
- **Revelation:** Switch Stance on another friendly.
- **While Witnessed:** +1 Sight/turn.

## Masked Usher — House edge
- **Veiled:** Enemy Forced Exposed elsewhere → Free Wager this.
- **Revelation:** If you control a Wagered Figure, gain 1 Sight.
- **Cash:** draw 1.

## Grinning Debtor — Bust specialist
- **Veiled:** First friendly Bust each turn → draw 1.
- **Revelation:** If Wagered, Blind Low; otherwise draw 1.
- **Cash:** gain 1 Sight.
