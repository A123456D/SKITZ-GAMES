# Ink Abyss — Wave 4 (closing pack · Ink 20)

Five cards. Completes the craft with **Dahaka** (Sovereign) and the remaining kit axes.

| Card | Type | Cost | Role |
|------|------|------|------|
| **Dahaka** | Figure · Sovereign | 5E / 3W · V3 / W7 | Craft legend |
| **Echo Blot** | Rite | 1E | Mill-Stain |
| **Blot Lens** | Relic | 2E · Graft | Blind payoff |
| **Stainwell** | Site | 2E | Enter-Stain / grind |
| **Abyss Urn** | Vessel | 3E / 2W · V1 / W3 | Continuity |

## Dahaka — Sovereign
- **SOVEREIGN** (≤1 in Constructed).
- **Veiled:** Whenever an enemy Figure becomes Forced Exposed, gain 1 Sight.
- **Revelation:** Stain each enemy Figure. If you Stained 2 or more, draw 1 and Blind Mid this turn.
- **While Witnessed:** Whenever you Forced Expose an enemy Figure, Blind that altitude this turn.
- **Fall:** Draw 1.

## Echo Blot — Mill-Stain
- Stain an enemy Figure in the chosen altitude if able. If Mid, draw 1. If it was already Stained, Blind that altitude this turn.

## Blot Lens — Blind payoff
- **Graft:** +1 power while host Witnessed.
- When you Blind an altitude that has a Stained enemy, gain 1 Sight. If that altitude is Low, also draw 1.

## Stainwell — Enter-Stain site
- **Site.** As you play this, Stain the enemy Veiled Figure here if able.
- Your Figures here have +1 power against Stained enemies.
- When a Stained enemy Figure here becomes Forced Exposed, gain 1 Sight.

## Abyss Urn — Vessel continuity
- **Vessel.** On play, tuck a Figure from hand as Inhabitant if able.
- **Revelation:** Release Inhabitant here if the lane is empty, otherwise to hand. Stain the enemy Figure here if able.
- **Fall:** release Inhabitant here Veiled if able.

## Pack sketch
Echo Blot / Stainwell seed Marks → Lens cashes Blinds → Urn refills after Fall → Dahaka turns every Erase into Sight or Blind and paints the board on Revelation.
