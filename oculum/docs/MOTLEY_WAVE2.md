# Motley Masquerade — Wave 2 (Trick support pack)

Five kit tools. Wave 1 keeps lane roles; Wave 2 funds Wagers and Stance.

| Card | Type | Cost | Role |
|------|------|------|------|
| **Scarlet Dealer** | Figure | 3E / 2W · V2 / W4 | Cash draw risk |
| **Velvet Antehall** | Site | 2E | Hall bank |
| **Masque Urn** | Vessel | 3E / 2W · V1 / W3 | Mask continuity |
| **Coinface Charm** | Relic | 2E · Graft | Flip graft |
| **Raise the Ante** | Rite | 1E | Paid ante push |

## Scarlet Dealer — Cash draw risk
- **Veiled:** When this Cashes, draw 1. May Wager.
- **Revelation:** Enter Stance B. If you control another Wagered Figure, draw 1.
- **Bust:** Opponent gains 1 Sight.

## Velvet Antehall — Hall bank
- **Site.** When a friendly Figure here Switches Stance, gain 1 Sight.
- When a friendly Figure here Cashes, gain 1 Sight.

## Masque Urn — Mask continuity
- **Vessel.** On play, tuck a Figure from hand as Inhabitant if able.
- **Revelation:** Free Wager this if able.
- When this Falls or is Forced Exposed: Free Wager another friendly Veiled Figure if able.

## Coinface Charm — Flip graft
- **Graft:** +1 power while host Witnessed.
- When host Switches Stance while Veiled, gain 1 Sight.
- When host Cashes, draw 1.

## Raise the Ante — Paid ante push
- Choose an altitude. Ante 1 Sight to Wager your Veiled Figure there if able.
- If it was already Wagered, Blind that altitude this turn.

## Pack sketch
Antehall banks Stance/Cash Sight → Charm pays flips into Sight → Raise pushes a paid ante → Dealer Cashes into cards → Urn still spreads Free Wager on break.
