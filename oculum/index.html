HOW TO PLAY

The idea

Things only fully exist when Witnessed. Figures enter Veiled (half-real, usually weaker). Spend Sight to Witness them — they become real, often hit harder, and fire a one-time Revelation.

Resources

Essence — pay to play cards from hand.

Sight — pay to Witness (yours or, with Gaze, an enemy's).

Will — your life (start at 30). Hit 0 and you lose by Break.

Three lanes

Each altitude has a standing Reveal rule — pick lanes on purpose.

HIGH — Gaze Witness costs 1 less Sight. Winning here deals +1 before soft damage.

MID — Witness your own figure here to draw 1.

LOW — Veiled figures get +1 power, but Witness and Gaze cost +1 Sight.

Reading a card

Essence — cost to play it.

Witness cost — Sight to make it Witnessed (the state of being real).

Veiled power — combat number while half-real.

Witnessed power — combat number once Witnessed. (Witnessed is a state; this number is separate.)

Witness vs Gaze

Witness — spend Sight on your Veiled card to reveal it.

Gaze — the same Witness action, but on an enemy Veiled card, so you steal their Revelation. You need a Gaze landmark on that lane (for example Iris Gaze).

Sites, Graft, Stance, Rites

Sites — landmarks on a lane (not fighters). They enter already Witnessed and give ongoing effects.

Graft — attach a Relic onto a Figure you control for extra power or triggers.

Stance — with a Third Face sigil on the lane, once per turn you can swap a figure's Veiled and Witnessed power numbers. Useful if you want the bigger number while still Veiled.

Rites — Blind a lane for the turn (no Sight income there). Starve their Sight to push Eclipse.

How you win

Break — reduce enemy Will to 0.

Eclipse — Motley Trick seals and card payoffs bank Eclipse. Reach 10 Eclipse to win without Breaking Will.

Time — after 10 rounds, highest Will wins.

When both players Pass, lanes Resolve: higher power chips Will. Veiled losers Hold (cannot die while Veiled). Witnessed losers Fall. Expose first to kill.

Controls

Drag a hand card onto a lane, or select it and tap the lane. Hold a card to inspect. Use Witness / Stance, then tap a glowing lane. New? Run First Gaze — it teaches card anatomy, Will / Sight / Eclipse, then a guided match.
