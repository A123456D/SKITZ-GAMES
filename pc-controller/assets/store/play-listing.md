## Short description
(80 characters max)

Bluetooth mouse and keyboard for PC, plus Wi‑Fi remotes for smart TVs.

## Full description

Pc Controller turns your Android phone into a remote for computers and smart TVs.

Control a Windows PC with a responsive Bluetooth touchpad and on-screen keyboard. No companion software is required on the computer. Pair once, keep the app open, and use the pad like a mouse with two-finger scroll and swipe-up keys.

For smart TVs on the same Wi‑Fi network, Pc Controller discovers and controls Roku, Samsung, LG, Bravia, and Android / Google TV with a dedicated remote layout. Samsung TVs can also be woken from standby with Power On with Mobile after one successful connection.

Features
• Bluetooth HID mouse and keyboard for PC
• Smooth touchpad with side scroll and swipe-up keyboard
• Wi‑Fi TV remotes for major smart TV platforms
• Remembers the last TV and reconnects automatically
• Samsung Wake-on-LAN power wake when supported
• In-app user manual

Requirements
• Android 9 or newer with Bluetooth HID Device support
• Same Wi‑Fi network for TV remotes
• Accept the TV pairing prompt on Samsung / LG when shown

Privacy
Pc Controller does not require an account and does not send your control activity to SKITZ servers. Device pairing information stays on your phone.
Privacy policy: https://skitz-games.pages.dev/apps/pc-controller/privacy/
