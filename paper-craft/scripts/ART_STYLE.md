# Paper Craft — art style bible (v4)

## Tone

Cool street-occult / anime-graphic stickers. **Not** childish stationery mascots.

## Brand lock (logo)

- Jagged die-cut white rim
- Crimson `#b01828` + matte black `#0e0c0e` + cream rim `#f4f0ea`
- Aggressive condensed type (Oswald), not handwriting
- Paper shards / torn silhouette energy

## Target bar

Riot Cube edgy + anime sticker craft (technique), cooler fantasy subjects.

## Must-haves

- Die-cut white sticker border
- Bold black outlines, marker texture fills
- High contrast, sharp silhouettes, readable at small size
- Solid black background for cutout
- Adult-cool mood: spirits, ronin, void fauna, runic beasts
- UI + card frames echo logo: dark stock, white rim, crimson accents

## Forbidden

- Cute baby animals, goofy grins, glue bottles, classroom props as the hero
- Soft washed doodles, photoreal, 3D gloss
- Soft cream stationery panels, washi-tape craft-table cute, cursive titles
